# NYPD Shooting Incident Insights

Source: `shootings_nyc.csv` with the NYPD shootings data dictionary. This incident table covers 2006-01-01 through 2026-06-30 and contains 24,310 unique incident records. The dictionary notes that victim and offender demographics are in separate related tables, so this analysis focuses on incident time and place.

## 1. Shootings fell for years, surged in 2020-2021, then declined again

Full-year incidents peaked at 1,566 in 2006 and reached a pre-pandemic low of 777 in 2019. Incidents then roughly doubled to an average of 1,547 in 2020-2021. By 2025, incidents were down to 688, a 56.0% decrease from 2021. The 2026 count is year-to-date through 2026-06-30, so it should not be compared to full years.

Chart: `outputs/01_yearly_trend.svg`

## 2. Brooklyn and the Bronx dominate the incident count

Brooklyn accounts for 9,777 incidents (40.2%) and the Bronx for 6,925 (28.5%). Together, they represent 68.7% of all recorded incidents. Queens and Manhattan are materially lower, while Staten Island is a small share.

Chart: `outputs/02_borough_totals.svg`

## 3. The risk window is heavily evening and late night

Evening hours from 6 p.m. to 11 p.m. account for 9,629 incidents (39.6%). The single busiest hours are 11 p.m. (1,999), 10 p.m. (1,919), and midnight (1,893). Hours 8 p.m. through 11 p.m. alone make up 29.6% of the dataset.

Chart: `outputs/03_hourly_pattern.svg`

## 4. Shootings are seasonal, with a clear warm-weather lift

June through September account for 42.4% of all incidents, higher than the one-third share expected if incidents were evenly distributed across months. July is the highest month at 2,778 incidents, followed by August at 2,699.

Chart: `outputs/04_monthly_pattern.svg`

## 5. A small set of precincts carries a large share

The top five precincts by incident count are 75, 73, 67, 44, 79; together they account for 22.8% of all incidents. Precinct 75 has the highest count at 1,395, followed by precinct 73 at 1,310.

Chart: `outputs/05_top_precincts.svg`

## 6. Incidents are overwhelmingly outdoor and street/housing-related

Outdoor incidents total 20,859, or 85.8% of all records. The `STREET` and `HOUSING` location classes together account for 83.9% of incidents, making them the dominant environmental context in this table.

Chart: `outputs/06_location_class.svg`

## Notes

- `LOCATION_DESC` is missing or unspecified for many street incidents, so it is less reliable as a primary categorization field than `LOC_CLASSFCTN_DESC`.
- Borough values `PBXN` and `PBXS` appear as small Patrol Borough categories rather than standard borough names; they were left unchanged in the totals.
- Charts were generated by `analyze_shootings.py`.
